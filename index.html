Support

Pray Together is a guided prayer app for praying in your own tradition, with text-to-speech or your own recorded voice.

Get help

Questions, problems, or a prayer you’d like to see in the app? Email support@praytogethersupport.com.

Common questions

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Options → Account → Delete Account. This deletes your account and its cloud data. It does not cancel a Pray Together Plus subscription; cancel that in your App Store or Google Play settings.

How do I cancel or restore Pray Together Plus?

Cancel in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. To restore a subscription on a new device, sign in and tap Restore Purchases under Options → Account.
